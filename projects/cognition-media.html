RESEARCH / 06

Cognition → Media

Turning academic questions about minds and machines into accessible public writing.

PROJECT

Research should travel.

Academic work can become difficult to access when its ideas stay inside specialist language. This ongoing project experiments with translating cognitive science, computationalism and technology research into visual essays and social-first pieces.

The aim is not to flatten difficult ideas, but to make the first doorway into them easier to find.

INTERESTS

Computational models of cognition.

Consciousness and representation.

Human–AI interaction.

Technology, power and surveillance.
