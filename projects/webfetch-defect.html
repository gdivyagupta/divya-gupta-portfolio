CASE STUDY / 02

Web Data Defect Analysis

Understanding where automated web research breaks — and how to design around those failures.

QUESTION

What happens between a page and a decision?

Automated research pipelines inherit the weaknesses of the web: duplicated listings, missing fields, inconsistent naming, outdated pages and ambiguous ownership.

The work focuses on mapping these defects, separating recoverable errors from hard failures, and creating validation steps before data enters downstream workflows.

PRINCIPLES

Normalize before comparing.

Keep provenance with every important field.

Validate high-impact records manually or with independent evidence.

Measure defect rates instead of assuming scraped data is clean.
