CASE STUDY / 05

AI Product & Dashboard Audit

Finding where interfaces can reduce cognitive load, surface the right signal and automate repetitive decisions.

LENS

Good dashboards answer questions.

Operational dashboards often accumulate metrics without clarifying what a user should do next. An audit combines information architecture, user goals, workflow analysis and automation opportunities.

The goal is to turn a collection of charts into a decision system: what matters, why it changed, what needs attention and what can safely happen automatically.

METHOD

Map decisions before adding metrics.

Separate monitoring from action.

Reduce repetitive cognitive work with automation.

Make exceptions and escalation paths explicit.
