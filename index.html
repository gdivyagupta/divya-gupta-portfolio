ABOUT

A hybrid background is the point.

I studied Cognitive Science and Psychology at the University of Toronto, with a focus on computational cognition and statistics. My work has since moved between AI product strategy, business development, automation, research and technical prototyping.

I like problems that don't fit neatly inside one discipline: how people reason with machines, how AI systems fail, how products shape behaviour, and how we can build technology without treating humans as an afterthought.

RESEARCH QUESTIONS

How do we make AI systems reliable when the environment itself is unreliable?

What can computational models tell us about human cognition and consciousness?

How do interfaces, algorithms and surveillance technologies reshape behaviour?

What does ethical AI look like when we take power, access and marginalised communities seriously?
