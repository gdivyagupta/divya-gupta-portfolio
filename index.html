about me

my work sits between disciplines.

i studied cognitive science and psychology at the university of toronto, with a focus on computational cognition and a minor in statistics. since then, my work has moved between ai product, technical prototyping, business development, research and education.

i am drawn to ai product management and ai solutions engineering because both ask for translation: between technical systems, human needs, uncertainty and business constraints.

long term, i want to pursue research in cognitive science while continuing to build, evaluate and govern ai systems in practice.
