about me

my work sits between disciplines.

i studied cognitive science and psychology at the university of toronto, with a focus on computational cognition and a minor in statistics.

today, i work at the intersection of applied ai, product and technical systems. i am especially interested in agentic workflows, evaluation, human-ai interaction and the design choices that determine how much autonomy a system should have.

long term, i want to pursue research in cognitive science while continuing to build, evaluate and govern ai systems in practice.
